
The Lorenz system , developed by Edward Lorenz in 1963, is a set of three nonlinear differential equations that model atmospheric convection and exhibit chaotic behavior, famously producing the butterfly-shaped Lorenz attractor. The system's future behavior is fully determined by its initial conditions, yet extremely sensitive to small changes in those conditions .  This sensitivity is popularly known as the butterfly effect.
