
Gumowski‑Mira Attractor

The Gumowski‑Mira Attractor is not just another fractal equation for drawing pretty pictures, but it does good doing just that.

The Gumowski‑Mira attractor was created in 1980 by I. Gumowski and C. Mira while at CERN in Geneva, Switzerland, they developed the system of equations to simulate the trajectories of sub‑atomic particles in a short cylindrical particle accelerator.
